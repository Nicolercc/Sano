# ADR-001: One-Week MVP Scope

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-07-21

## Context

The team has one week and three contributors. The full product vision includes ingestion, scoring, maps, profiles, methodology, alternatives, and future database automation.

Attempting the entire long-term vision would increase delivery risk and reduce polish.

## Decision

The MVP will focus on one polished demo path:

1. Search/list/map page.
2. Restaurant cards with Sano labels.
3. Restaurant profile.
4. Inspection timeline.
5. Score panel.
6. Alternatives.
7. Methodology page.
8. Real official data or a curated real-data seed.

The MVP will not include accounts, saved lists, owner dashboards, payments, ML, review scraping, or nationwide coverage.

## Consequences

The demo can be completed in one week with stronger quality and clearer storytelling.

Some backend automation may remain future work.
